Sulaymaniyah Polytechnic University · Graduation project 2025–2026

We taught a neural network to design antennas.

Give it a patch size and it predicts how the antenna behaves. Give it a frequency between 1 and 7 GHz and it suggests the size to build. It learned from CST simulations, and it is tested against them.

How it works

1

Choose

Pick one of 12 patch shapes and type a size, or type the frequency you want to reach.

2

Predict

The engine returns the S11 and VSWR curves, the resonance and the bandwidth.

3

Check

Built-in self-tests compare the engine with held-out CST simulations.
